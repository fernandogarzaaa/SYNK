## What was removed

The pre-2.0 suite (`benchmark/agents.py`, `benchmark/scenarios.py`, the old
`runner.py`) compared four "agents" (naive / DOM / beta / WebMCP) against
mock page nodes with **hard-coded per-agent timings** (ttfa 0.5 / 0.3 /
0.2 / 0.1 seconds). Its "rankings" were arithmetic on invented constants,
not measurements, and its `results.json` was a stale artifact of that
fiction. All of it was deleted. The old `benchmark/metrics.py`
(TaskMetrics / AES score machinery) was replaced with two honest helpers
(`mean`, `percentile`); `harness/benchmarks.py` (used by the server's
`/benchmark` endpoint) is unchanged and documented below.

## Data-source labels

- **MEASURED**: wall-clock timing on this machine, this run.
- **COMPUTED**: deterministic outcome of real harness logic (no timing).
- **UNMEASURED**: not measured here; no number is reported.

## The server `/benchmark` endpoint (unchanged)

`GET /benchmark` runs three synthetic headless tasks via
`harness/benchmarks.py`: form-fill (real ContextManager + Orchestrator
plan), conflict avoidance (real TransactionRunner against a human-owned
target), workflow reuse (real WorkflowMiner/Memory). Its `tokens` are
estimates and `cost_usd` assumes $0.50/1M tokens; treat those two fields
as estimates, everything else as executed.

## What these numbers do NOT say

- Nothing here measures a live browser. Interaction latency, real-page
  verification rates, and Playwright/CDP adapter behavior are UNMEASURED.
- Throughput and latency are single-machine, unloaded measurements.
  They bound harness overhead; they are not production SLOs.
- The verifier accuracy suite tests decision logic against declared
  pairs; adversarial robustness is covered separately by
  `tests/test_adversarial.py` (40 tests).
